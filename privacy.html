Summary: Flapper stores your data on your device and your personal iCloud. We do not run servers that store your personal data. Free users: no personal data ever leaves your device. Premium AI features send only the context needed for each response through a Yong Lee proxy to Anthropic — an AI provider (LLM) — not to Yong Lee.

1. Who We Are

Flapper is developed and operated by Yong Lee. For questions, contact us via GitHub Issues.

2. Data We Collect and How We Use It

Location Data

Flapper requests background location access to learn the places you visit regularly (home, work, frequent stops). This data is:

  • Processed entirely on your device using on-device machine learning
  • Never transmitted to Yong Lee's servers or any third party
  • Backed up to your personal iCloud Drive when you enable backup

Photos and Camera

When you take a Snap check-in, the photo is stored locally on your device and optionally saved to your photo library. Photos are never uploaded to external servers.

Medication and Health Notes

Medication logs, tags, and notes you enter are stored locally using SwiftData and backed up to your personal iCloud Drive. This data is only transmitted externally when you use the AI Medication Report feature (see Section 3).

Routines and Anchor Events

Your routines, anchor events, and milestones are stored locally on your device and backed up to your personal iCloud Drive.

Calendar and Reminders

Flapper reads your calendar and reminders to let you choose today's anchor event. This data is read locally and never transmitted externally.

Focus Mode

Flapper reads your iOS Focus Mode status (e.g., Work, Sleep, Personal) to adjust activity predictions and suppress non-urgent reminders. Focus Mode data is:

  • Processed exclusively on-device
  • Never transmitted to external servers
  • Stored locally and backed up to your personal iCloud Drive

3. AI Features and Third-Party Services

Free users: No personal data leaves your device.

Premium users: AI features send context through a developer-operated proxy server (adht-proxy.aumosita.workers.dev) to Anthropic, an AI company (LLM provider). Yong Lee is not an AI company and does not have access to the content of your requests — Anthropic is the AI service provider. The proxy forwards requests without storing your data. Each AI feature sends only the context needed to generate its response:

Anchor Suggestions, Checklists, Nudges, End-of-Day Encouragement

  • Anchor event titles and times
  • Routine names and recent completion status
  • Learned place names (e.g., "Home", "Work") — not GPS coordinates
  • Calendar event titles and times
  • Conversation memory notes you have created
  • Current Focus Mode status (e.g., "Work", "Sleep")

Medication Report (Premium)

When you generate an AI medication report, the following is sent to Anthropic via the proxy:

  • Medication name
  • Dose summaries (dates and times of logged doses)
  • Effect and side-effect tags you have entered

GPS coordinates and photos are never included in any AI request. Anthropic's data handling is governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

4. iCloud Backup

When you enable backup, Flapper stores an encrypted backup file in your personal iCloud Drive container. This backup is accessible only to you and is subject to Apple's iCloud terms. Yong Lee cannot access your iCloud data.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage telemetry
  • We do not run advertising networks
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties
  • We do not create user accounts or store data on our servers

6. Children's Privacy

Flapper is rated 9+ and is not directed at children under 9. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your Rights

Because your data lives on your device and your personal iCloud, you control it entirely. You can delete all app data by deleting the app. You can delete your iCloud backup from iCloud Drive at any time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions or concerns? Open an issue on GitHub.

앵커 제안, 체크리스트, 넛지, 하루 마무리 격려

  • 앵커 이벤트 제목과 시간

3. AI機能とサードパーティサービス

無料ユーザー：個人情報はデバイスの外に送信されません。

プレミアムユーザー：AI機能は、開発者が運営するプロキシサーバー（adht-proxy.aumosita.workers.dev）を通じて、LLMプロバイダーであるAnthropic（Claude API）にリクエストを転送します。Yong LeeはAI会社ではなく、リクエストの内容にアクセスしません — AIサービスはAnthropicが提供しています。プロキシはデータを保存せずリクエストを中継するのみです。各AI機能は応答生成に必要なコンテキストのみを送信します：

アンカー提案・チェックリスト・ナッジ・一日の締めくくり

  • アンカーイベントのタイトルと時間
  • ルーティン名と最近の完了状況

  • カレンダーイベントのタイトルと時間
  • ユーザーが作成した会話メモリの内容
  • 現在の集中モード状態（例：「仕事」「睡眠」）

AI服薬レポート（プレミアム）

AI服薬レポートを生成する際、以下の情報がプロキシ経由でAnthropicに送信されます：

  • 薬の名前

  • 入力した効果・副作用タグ
